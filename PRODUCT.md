# Product

## Register

product

## Users

IHBA staff and trusted editors maintain bilingual institutional content, projects,
news, board membership, media, and incoming enquiries. They need a dependable
workspace that makes routine publishing quick without requiring technical
knowledge.

## Product Purpose

The product gives IHBA one secure source of truth for its public website and
operational inboxes. Success means staff can update English and Turkish content,
publish projects and news, and process submissions without editing source code,
while the existing public presentation remains unchanged.

## Brand Personality

Calm, accountable, and humane. The interface should feel institutional without
being bureaucratic, and confident without being decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style dashboards, oversized metric cards, decorative gradients,
rounded SaaS surfaces, page-builder complexity, and unfamiliar interaction
patterns. The admin must not visually leak into or restyle public pages.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve public trust by keeping the current public UI stable.
2. Put the next editorial action in reach without hiding core controls.
3. Make bilingual completeness visible before publishing.
4. Prefer explicit states and recoverable actions over clever automation.
5. Keep operational data dense, legible, and easy to scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels,
clear validation, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, and responsive workflows
for laptop and mobile use.
