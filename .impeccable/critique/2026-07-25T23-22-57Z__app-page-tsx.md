## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: "AI-assisted template, human-tuned." Tells: AI-sheen documentary photos presented as field documentation; uppercase ✦ marquee; floating confetti squares; identical icon-card anatomy across two sections (9 cards, 1 template); hero-metric stats for non-impact numbers. Saves: arch motif from logo (real identity decision), disciplined navy/gold/azure palette, eyebrows confirmed removed, no gradient text/glassmorphism, systematic radii.

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs --json app` → 0 findings (exit 0). Code-level slop patterns absent.

**Browser visualization**: skipped for time; fallback = prior Playwright screenshot evidence (v3-hero/v3-cta/v3-mobile + section shots), live-verified.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Donate funnel is a dead end** — 3 CTAs (header/hero/CTA band) all resolve to #contact footer (hotmail + GSM). No IBAN/form. Fix: interim WhatsApp/mailto panel with prefilled subject + honest copy ("Bağış için bize ulaşın"); own #donate section when accounts exist. Suggested: $impeccable harden
- **[P0] Newsletter fakes success** — setSubmitted(true) client-side, data evaporates. Dishonest feedback poisons all page claims. Fix: Formspree/Buttondown endpoint or mailto:. Suggested: $impeccable harden
- **[P1] Synthetic photoreal imagery as field docs** — esp. Afghan classroom for a planning-stage school; donor-savvy visitors clock the AI sheen. Fix: 2-3 real photos (board, office, Mazar-i-Sharif land) or caption as visualization. Suggested: $impeccable bolder
- **[P1] 16 dead href="#" links** — 5 social icons + 11 footer links read as "no presence, no reports" against the transparency claim. Fix: real URLs or remove. Suggested: $impeccable distill
- **[P2] FactsStrip parades vanity numbers as impact** — "2025/7/3/2" at text-7xl borrows outcome-metric grammar. Fix: reframe as identity strip, smaller weight, or honest early signals. Suggested: $impeccable layout
